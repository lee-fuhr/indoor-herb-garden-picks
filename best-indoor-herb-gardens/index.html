Quick answer

The AeroGarden Harvest is the best indoor herb garden for most people, offering a near-perfect balance of size, ease, and reliability.

The AeroGarden Bounty is a strong runner-up for those who want more capacity, the Click and Grow Smart Garden 3 wins for absolute simplicity, the Back to the Roots kit is the budget-friendly pick, and the AeroGarden Farm XL is the powerhouse for serious growers.

Growing fresh herbs indoors year-round used to be a fiddly project involving pots, dirt, and prayer. Today’s countertop gardens do the heavy lifting: they add full-spectrum LED lights, automatic watering, and in some cases even tell you when to refill the tank. The result is a steady supply of basil, mint, cilantro, and more, right in your kitchen. When choosing an indoor herb garden, focus on three things: pod count (how many plants you can grow at once), light quality (most use LEDs with a timer), and water capacity (how often you’ll top off the tank). Size and noise from any pump also matter. The models below cover the range from tiny three-pod systems to near-professional twenty-four-pod setups, so you can pick the one that fits your counter and your appetite for fresh herbs.

Best for specific needs

Best for small spaces

If your kitchen counter is tight, the AeroGarden Harvest is the obvious choice. It’s compact enough to sit beside the coffee maker yet grows six herbs, plenty for daily cooking. You won’t sacrifice growth for size; the LED light and pump are well-matched to the pod count, so you get full, bushy plants without the bulk. Our pick: AeroGarden Harvest Indoor Garden.

Best for sizable harvests

For those who go through basil and mint like water, the AeroGarden Bounty is a clear upgrade. Nine pods mean you can grow multiple varieties at once, and the larger water tank cuts down on refills. The touchscreen adds convenience, but the real draw is the extra growing room without jumping to a floor-standing unit. Our pick: AeroGarden Bounty Indoor Garden 9-Pod.

Best for family fun

The Back to the Roots kit turns herb gardening into a living ecosystem with a tiny fish. It’s inexpensive, visually fascinating, and teaches kids about cycles in nature. The herbs grow well enough for garnishes and teas, and the whole setup fits on a desk or windowsill. It’s less about high yields and more about inspiring a love of growing. Our pick: Back to the Roots Indoor Herb Garden Kit.

Our verdict

For most households, the AeroGarden Harvest hits the sweet spot of size, reliability, and value. But if you need more capacity, the Bounty is a worthy upgrade, or if you want a unique experience on a budget, the Back to the Roots kit is a delightful alternative. The Click and Grow excels for beginners who want ultimate simplicity, while the Farm XL is a powerhouse for serious greenery. No matter your space or skill level, there’s an indoor herb garden here that will keep your kitchen stocked year-round.
